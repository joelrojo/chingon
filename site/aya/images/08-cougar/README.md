**The four sightings** are the joke: same person, four centuries, same expression. Treat each as
period-correct evidence, not a costume.

- **1603 Kyoto** — Ukiyo-e-adjacent woodblock scene, indigo and ochre, worn paper. One small
  figure clearly her. Treatment `faded`.
- **1872 Mt. Fuji** — Albumen-print look, soft focus, Fuji in haze. A figure on the trail who is
  unmistakably Aya. Treatment `sepia`.
- **1987 Japan** — Real film photo, bilateral peace sign. Date stamp stays in frame. No filter. Aspect `3 / 2`.
- **2026 Los Angeles** — Temporary real photo (`animals/portrait-with-cat.jpg`, was 999B30F9). Focal `{ x: 62, y: 32 }` keeps both faces in the 4/3 crop.
- **1998** — Not on the page. The slot file is still there if the rave comes back.
